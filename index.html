Welcome to Public Welfare Trust Araria (PWTA). We are a non-profit organization committed to serving humanity and creating a positive impact in our community.

Our primary focus is on providing quality education to underprivileged children and organizing health camps for those in need.

We believe in women's empowerment and regularly conduct skill development programs for them.

This platform is designed to bring transparency and trust to our operations.

Here, you can view our recent activities and track our financial records in real-time.

The 'Revenue' and 'Expenditure' sections allow our members to maintain a clear record of donations received and funds utilized.

If you wish to support our cause, you can scan the QR code at the bottom to donate directly.

Thank you for visiting, and together, let's make a difference!
